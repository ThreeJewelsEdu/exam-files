Exam Instructions & TCS iON Railway Pattern

1. Examination Structure: Clock shows time remaining. In Overall Timer mode (standard for NTPC, Group D, and RPF), candidates can navigate freely across all subjects. In Sectional Timer mode (such as ALP Part A & B), modules run in a fixed sequence and lock permanently once expired or submitted.

2. Exact 1/3rd Negative Marking: Follows official Railway rules. For each wrong answer, exactly 1/3rd (one-third) of the marks assigned to that question will be deducted (i.e. -0.33 for 1-mark questions, -0.67 for 2-mark questions).

3. Official 5-Color TCS iON Legend:

  • Green: You have answered the question.
  • Red: You have visited but not answered the question.
  • Purple: Marked for Review (Unanswered).
  • Purple with Green Dot: Answered & Marked for Review. This will be evaluated in the final merit!
  • Gray: You have not visited the question yet.

4. Qualifying Modules: Sections marked with a "Qualifying Module" badge (e.g. RRB ALP Part B Trade Test) have a qualifying cut-off (e.g. 35%) and are excluded from the Grand Merit calculation.
