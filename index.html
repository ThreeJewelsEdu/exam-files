Frequently Asked Questions

Are the doubt sessions and mentorship really free right now?

Yes! As part of TRIRATNA's compassionate learning initiative, our doubt-clearing desk and personal mentorship appointments are currently 100% free of charge for all serious aspirants.

Why does the appointment require booking 4 days in advance?

Our faculty members dedicate focused, uninterrupted time to every single student. The 4-day minimum window allows mentors to review your target exam requirements and prepare a personalized roadmap before meeting you.

Which exams do you provide guidance for?

We specialize in 4 core verticals: Banking (including RBI Exam, SBI, IBPS), Staff Selection Commission (SSC CGL/CHSL/MTS/CPO), Railways (RRB NTPC, ALP, JE, Group D), and exclusively the UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE).
