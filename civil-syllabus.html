Detailed Examination Structure

Switch between stages to review eligibility, syllabus, and paper patterns.

Stage I: Preliminary Examination (Objective Screening)

Both papers are conducted on the same day. Marks in Prelims are screening only and not counted in final merit.

GS Paper I 200 Marks | 2 Hours

General Studies 1

  • 100 Multiple Choice Questions (2 marks each).
  • Negative marking: 1/3rd (0.66 marks) deducted per incorrect answer.
  • Syllabus: Indian Polity, History of India & INM, Geography, Economic & Social Development, Environment & Ecology, General Science, Current Events of National & International Importance.
  • Determines the cut-off score for qualifying to Mains.
GS Paper II 200 Marks | 2 Hours

CSAT (Civil Services Aptitude Test)

  • 80 Multiple Choice Questions (2.5 marks each).
  • Negative marking: 1/3rd (0.83 marks) deducted per incorrect answer.
  • Syllabus: Reading Comprehension, Interpersonal & Communication Skills, Logical Reasoning & Analytical Ability, Decision-Making & Problem-Solving, Basic Numeracy (Class X level).
  • Qualifying nature: Candidate must score at least 33% (66 marks).

Stage II: Mains Examination (Written Descriptive)

Consists of 9 conventional subjective papers. Total Merit marks = 1,750.

Qualifying Papers (Not counted in final ranking)

Paper A (300 Marks): One of the Indian Languages included in the 8th Schedule of the Constitution (25% qualifying).
Paper B (300 Marks): English Language Comprehension and Précis writing (25% qualifying).
Paper Subject Marks
Paper I Essay (Two essays from diverse themes) 250
Paper II General Studies I (Indian Heritage & Culture, History & Geography of the World, Society) 250
Paper III General Studies II (Governance, Constitution, Polity, Social Justice & International Relations) 250
Paper IV General Studies III (Technology, Economic Development, Biodiversity, Environment, Security & Disaster Management) 250
Paper V General Studies IV (Ethics, Integrity, and Aptitude — Case Studies) 250
Paper VI Optional Subject — Paper 1 250
Paper VII Optional Subject — Paper 2 250
Total Written Mains Score Considered for Merit 1,750 Marks

Stage III: Personality Test / Interview (275 Marks)

Conducted at Dholpur House, UPSC headquarters in New Delhi by a board of competent observers.

Purpose of the Board

Not a test of rote knowledge (already tested in Mains), but an assessment of mental alertness, critical assimilation powers, clear and logical exposition, and moral integrity.

DAF Assessment

Detailed questions from the candidate's Detailed Application Form (DAF): home state, educational background, hobbies, career choices, and optional subject.

Final Ranking Computation

Mains Written Exam (1,750 Marks) + Personality Test (275 Marks) = Grand Total: 2,025 Marks used for final All India Rank (AIR).

Eligibility Criteria & Number of Attempts

Official guidelines as notified by the Union Public Service Commission.

Educational Qualification

A degree from a Central, State, or Deemed University, or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Government of India. Candidates in final year may appear for Prelims.

Age Limit (as on 1st August)

Minimum Age: 21 Years.
General / EWS: Up to 32 Years.
OBC: Up to 35 Years.
SC / ST: Up to 37 Years.

Number of Permissible Attempts

General / EWS: 6 Attempts.
OBC: 9 Attempts.
SC / ST: Unlimited (within age limit).
PwBD: 9 attempts for Gen/OBC.
