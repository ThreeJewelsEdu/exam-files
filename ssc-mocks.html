Staff Selection Commission Exam Instructions

1. Two timer modes:

  • Overall timer: One countdown for the whole paper. You can move freely across all sections.
  • Sectional timer: Each section has its own clock. A section locks when its time ends or when you lock it early, and locked sections cannot be revisited.

2. Marking scheme: The marks for each question are shown at the top of the question. Qualifying modules are scored separately and do not count toward merit, but you must reach the stated cut-off.

3. Palette symbols:

  • Green: Answered.
  • Red: Visited but not answered.
  • Purple: Marked for review without an answer.
  • Purple with green dot: Answered and marked. This answer is evaluated.
  • Gray: Not visited yet.

4. Shortcuts: ← and → move between questions. A–E select an option.

5. Tab switching: Leaving the exam screen is counted and shown in your scorecard.
